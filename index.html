Wander Around

Meet our beautiful neighbors

Our restaurant lives in harmony with the environment. Before or after your meal, you are warmly invited to walk the grounds and share a quiet moment with the family.

The Swan Pool

A expansive, crystal-clear natural pool reserved entirely for our elegant resident white swans. A peaceful sight right next to our outdoor terrace.

Our Friendly Pony

Always curious and incredibly gentle, our resident pony loves greeting guests (especially the little ones) near the orchard meadow.

Free-Roaming Chickens

Wandering happily through the clover fields, our heritage hens contribute directly to our daily breakfast recipes and fresh bakes.

Rooted in Earth

An experience that feels like coming home.

We built this space to slow down time. Our kitchen is driven by the seasons—gathering what we grow right here and cooking it over open fires or serving it raw and vibrant.

Whether you sit under the shade of our old oak trees watching the swans drift by, or warm up by the indoor hearth, you're treated like old friends coming over for Sunday supper.
